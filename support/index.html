Alerts

I am not getting delay or gate alerts.

Check that notifications are allowed for FlightPal in your phone’s Settings, and that the alert types you want are on in FlightPal › Settings › Notifications. Alerts follow the airline’s own data, so a gate appears only once the airport publishes it.

How early does tracking start?

Add a flight any time after it is scheduled. Live status appears as the airline and airport publish it, usually in the day before departure.

FlightPal Pro

What does Pro include?

Unlimited tracked flights and full history, the compensation checker and claim letter, Track My Aircraft, connection tracking, calendar sync, family sharing, the home-screen widget and cloud backup. Delay, gate and cancellation alerts are free for everyone.

How do I cancel or get a refund?

Subscriptions are billed and managed by the store you bought through. On iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › FlightPal. On Android: Google Play › Payments & subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google under their policies.

I bought Pro on a new phone and it is not showing.

Open the Pro screen and tap Restore purchases, signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you subscribed with.

Accounts and backup

Do I need an account?

No. Sign in with Apple or Google only if you want to back up your flights and restore them on another phone.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings › Account & backup › Delete account. This permanently removes your account and its data from our server. It does not cancel a subscription. See Delete your account for details.

Compensation

Will FlightPal claim compensation for me?

No. FlightPal checks whether a delay or cancellation looks eligible under UK261 or EU261 and prepares a letter you send to the airline yourself. Airlines can refuse for extraordinary circumstances such as weather or air-traffic-control restrictions; FlightPal says so rather than promise money.
