Delete Your Account

Last updated 26 September 2026

The short version

In the app, go to Settings › Account & backup › Delete account. Your account and its server data are deleted immediately. If you cannot use the app, email us and we will do it within 30 days. If you never signed in, there is no account to delete — just uninstall the app.

1. Delete in the app

Open FlightPal, go to Settings › Account & backup, tap Delete account and confirm. If you signed in with Apple, you are asked to confirm with your Apple ID so FlightPal’s access can be revoked. You are signed out when deletion completes.

2. Or request deletion by email

Send an email to nicholas.orr099@gmail.com from the email address linked to your Apple or Google sign-in, with the subject “FlightPal account deletion request”. We will confirm once your account has been deleted, within 30 days.

3. What gets deleted

  • Your account record (the opaque identifier from your Apple or Google sign-in).
  • Your encrypted cloud backup, if you used one.
  • Share links you created.
  • Flight alert subscriptions tied to your account, and their event history.
  • Our cached copy of your subscription status, and RevenueCat’s customer record for your account.

4. What is not affected

Flight history, claim drafts and app preferences stored on your phone stay there, and the app keeps working without an account. Uninstalling the app removes them. Deleting your account does not cancel a Pro subscription — cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.
