Delete Your Account

Last updated 3 August 2026

The short version

Email us and we'll delete your account and any backup data within 30 days. If you never signed in, there is no account to delete — just uninstall the app.

1. How to request deletion

Send an email to nicholas.orr099@gmail.com from the email address linked to your Apple or Google sign-in, with the subject “FlightPal account deletion request”. We will confirm once your account and any stored backup have been deleted.

2. What gets deleted

  • Your account record (the opaque identifier and, if provided, the email address associated with your Apple or Google sign-in).
  • Your encrypted backup snapshot, if you ever used cloud backup.
  • Your device’s notification token and alert preferences.

Deletion is completed within 30 days of a verified request.

3. What stays on your device

Flight history, claim drafts and app preferences are stored locally on your device and are not affected by an account-deletion request. Uninstalling the app removes them, or you can clear individual tracked flights from within the app at any time.

4. If you never signed in

Sign-in is optional in FlightPal. If you have never signed in with Apple or Google, no account exists on our server — uninstalling the app removes everything FlightPal stored on your device.
